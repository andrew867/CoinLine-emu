# Hardware reference checklist: evidence review

## Assessment

The firmware-facing handoffs answer the principal register, timing, state and acceptance questions well enough to continue several emulator implementations. They do **not** replace a schematic, connector electrical pinout, physical measurement, component identification or revision-specific qualification. The requests in the [hardware reference checklist](hardware-reference-request-checklist.md) should remain open wherever they ask for such evidence.

The following review adds the physical evidence already available in product and service publications. Page numbers are the printed document pages, not the PDF viewer's page index. The documents are identified by publication title, date, issue and figure so the evidence can be located and reviewed without reproducing the manuals.

## Highest-value remaining physical references

1. ADSI graphical daughterboard: clear photographs of both faces with chip markings and connector labels, or original board drawings. Correlate its analogue input and modem status timing with the current initial-response trace.
2. Original and later alert/data-jack boards: full part identification, schematics or benign signal captures. The older fixed-frequency alert and later timer-generated alert must be separate profiles.
3. Desktop unit: after repair, a reference photo of the data-jack board, card reader, display and the identifiable board revision, then a normal isolated modem-call and reminder capture. The physical manual already gives an excellent starting point.
4. Actual 11-line panel assembly and reader labels: a manufacturer reference or datasheet tied to those specific parts, not a visually similar 2-line module.
5. Reference board diagrams and normal supply/reset/relay timing measurements. Treat inferred component functions, documented target specs, actual measurements and emulator acceptance as different evidence classes.

No item requiring a complete physical schematic, pinout or full native qualification has been marked complete solely on the strength of this documentation review.
